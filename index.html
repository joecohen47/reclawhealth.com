How Reclaw works

Reclaw connects to the practice system and the payer portals, the same places a biller works today.

01 · Triage

Every claim that needs attention, already analyzed.

Denials, rejections, claims the payer never answered, and payments to post, each one row with what the payer said, the likely cause and the next step.

  • Filters for Urgent, Approve, Needs you and Waiting
  • Simple fixes can be approved straight from the list
02 · One approval

A complete plan, with the source for every change.

Correct and resubmit, appeal through the payer portal with records attached, post the payment, bill the client or the other plan. One approval covers every step.

  • Each field change cites the exact payer remark or chart line
  • Code checks every quote: an invented reason shows as "not found"
  • Letters are written first, so you approve the actual text
03 · Your judgment

It knows when a person has to decide.

Did the client agree to pay? Which record is right? When a call belongs to a person, Reclaw asks one question with a few answers, then carries out the one you pick.

  • Routing comes from the payer's codes and written rules, not from the model
  • Every result is checked in the practice system or portal afterward

Built so a biller can trust it.

Reclaw does the work, but nothing reaches a payer or a client without a person's approval, and every action leaves a record.

  • Nothing happens without approvalEvery plan waits for the biller. Anything with a warning never gets a one-click approve.
  • Checked after every stepOnly the approved fields changed, exactly one submission, the right documents attached.
  • See what it didEach page the agent opened and the exact text it typed, kept on the claim's history.
  • Hard rules it won't breakIt never bills a client for an amount the payer says the practice owns, and never appeals a closed window.
  • A stop switchOne switch stops all agents. Anything handled outside Reclaw can be marked completed manually.
  • An audit recordAn append-only log of everything people and Reclaw did, ready to export.
